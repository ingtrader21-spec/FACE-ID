Dominican Republic enrollment

A person scans the QR, enters their name and Dominican cédula reference, takes a face photo, and explicitly consents to enrollment.

  1. Scan this QR on the same local network.
  2. Enter full name and cédula.
  3. Take a clear front-facing photo.
  4. Accept biometric consent and submit.
Privacy design: the full cédula number is hashed on the phone before submission. FACE-ID stores only the cryptographic reference and the last four digits in the enrollment reference. This is not Dominican government identity verification.
